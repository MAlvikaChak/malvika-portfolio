Cars24 Elite

Strategic interior design and spatial planning for a luxury automotive retail experience, focusing on high-end customer journeys and brand immersion.

target The Challenge

Transforming the traditional used-car buying experience into a premium, frictionless journey. The objective was to design a flagship physical space that aligns with Cars24's digital-first ethos while exuding luxury and trust. The space needed to accommodate both vehicle display and high-end consultation zones without feeling cluttered.

architecture Spatial Strategy

We implemented a zoned approach, transitioning from high-energy display areas to intimate, acoustic-treated negotiation lounges. Using a minimalist material palette of brushed steel, dark oak, and subtle LED linear lighting, we created an environment that highlights the vehicles while providing a comfortable, VIP experience for the clientele.
